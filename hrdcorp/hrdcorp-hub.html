HRDC Claimable Programs

Whether you are looking for specific public programs or tailored in-house training, all our solutions are designed to meet HRD Corp requirements.

What is Claimable with HRD Corp Support*:

Funding is available for training with a minimum duration of four (4) hours, covering:

  • Meal Allowance
  • Daily Allowance
  • Consumable Training Materials
  • Hotel or Venue Rental
  • Airfare or Transportation

*Please refer to the Allowable Cost Matrix for detailed expenses.
