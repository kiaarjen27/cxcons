What Is Claimable with HRD Corp Support*

Funding support is available for eligible training with a minimum duration of four (4) hours. Depending on the scheme and Allowable Cost Matrix, covered items may include:

  • Meal Allowance
  • Daily Allowance
  • Consumable Training Materials
  • Hotel or Venue Rental
  • Airfare or Transportation

*Please refer to the Allowable Cost Matrix for detailed expense eligibility.
