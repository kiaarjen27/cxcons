Program overview

ESG Awareness gives professionals a clear working understanding of environmental, social, and governance principles. Participants learn carbon footprint concepts, Scope 1–3 emissions, GRI-related reporting ideas, and how sustainability frameworks support organisational decision-making.

Designed for organisations starting or strengthening their ESG journey — including SMEs and corporate teams.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand ESG principles clearly
  • Calculate carbon footprint accurately
  • Identify Scope 1–3 emissions
  • Apply GRI reporting standards
  • Develop sustainability frameworks

Who this is for

  • Managers, engineers, executives, and sustainability officers
  • SMEs aligning with sustainability goals
  • Professionals driving organisational impact

Why organisations choose this program

ESG expectations from customers, regulators, and stakeholders continue to rise. Teams need a shared language and practical understanding before strategy, reporting, or operational changes can succeed.
