Program overview

This program builds payroll capability from core concepts through practical calculations and compliance-focused handling of wages, overtime, leave, and deductions. Participants strengthen accuracy and confidence in day-to-day payroll operations and reporting discipline.

Suitable for HR, payroll, finance, and business owners who need reliable payroll control aligned with Malaysian employment requirements.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand wages vs salary
  • Apply Employment Act provisions
  • Calculate overtime accurately
  • Manage leave entitlements
  • Handle payroll deductions

Who this is for

  • HR managers and payroll officers
  • Finance and compliance professionals
  • Business owners managing employees

Why organisations choose this program

Payroll errors create compliance risk, employee disputes, and rework. Clear process knowledge reduces mistakes and strengthens audit readiness.

Especially useful when teams need consistent handling of overtime, leave, and statutory-related payroll items.
