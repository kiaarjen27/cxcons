Program overview

Basics of First Aider (BOFA), CPR and AED builds foundational emergency response capability. Participants learn how to recognise critical situations, perform CPR and AED procedures correctly, and manage common injuries with structured, calm action.

Designed for workplace first responders and safety teams who need practical competence that supports organisational emergency readiness.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand basic life support
  • Perform CPR and AED correctly
  • Manage an unconscious victim safely
  • Identify fractures and wounds
  • Treat burns and scalds

Who this is for

  • Safety officers and OSH practitioners
  • First aiders and emergency response staff
  • Manufacturing and service industry employees

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Early, correct response can reduce harm before professional help arrives. Training first aiders strengthens workplace preparedness and supports a stronger safety culture.

Useful for organisations building or refreshing internal first-response capability across operations and facilities.
