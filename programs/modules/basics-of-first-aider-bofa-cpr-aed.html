Program overview

This program builds foundational first aid capability for workplace responders. Participants learn how to recognise emergencies, perform CPR and AED procedures correctly, and manage common injuries with calm, structured action.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand basic life support
  • Perform CPR and AED correctly
  • Manage unconscious victim safely
  • Identify fractures and wounds
  • Treat burns and scalds

Prepared for

  • Safety officers and OSH practitioners
  • First aiders and emergency response staff
  • Manufacturing and service industry employees
