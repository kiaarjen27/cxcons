Program overview

This program helps participants distinguish accounting and finance roles in receivables work, apply credit management principles, assess AR operations, improve billing and collection processes, and strengthen AR risk management.

Designed for finance, credit, sales support, and operations teams involved in the order-to-cash cycle.

Learning outcomes

  • Distinguish accounting vs finance
  • Apply credit management principles
  • Assess AR operations effectively
  • Improve billing and collection processes
  • Strengthen AR risk management

Who this is for

  • Finance and AR managers
  • Sales and credit executives
  • Administrative and operations managers

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Cash flow depends on disciplined credit and receivables practice. Teams that share the same standards for assessment, billing, collection, and escalation reduce leakage and dispute delays.

A practical finance operations module with direct commercial impact.
