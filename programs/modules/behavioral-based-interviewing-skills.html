Program overview

Behavioral Based Interviewing Skills helps supervisors and HR professionals move beyond generic interviews. Participants learn how to apply behavioural methods, design role-specific questions, probe evidence, and evaluate adaptability, teamwork, and communication more consistently.

Built for leaders who want fairer, clearer hiring decisions based on past performance indicators.

Learning outcomes

  • Apply behavioral interview methods
  • Customize role-specific questions
  • Probe candidate strengths and weaknesses
  • Assess adaptability under pressure
  • Evaluate team and communication skills

Who this is for

  • Supervisors, officers, executives, and managers
  • HR professionals conducting interviews
  • Leaders improving hiring decisions

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Poor interviews create costly hiring mistakes. A shared behavioural method improves consistency across interviewers and strengthens the quality of selection decisions.

Useful for growing teams, high-volume hiring, and managers who interview occasionally but need stronger structure.
