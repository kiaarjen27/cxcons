Program overview

This program builds foundational awareness of ISO 9001 quality management principles. Participants learn how risk-based thinking works, how to identify risks and opportunities, and how day-to-day operations can align with a quality management system that supports continuous improvement.

Designed for organisations introducing QMS awareness across departments, not only quality specialists.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand ISO 9001 principles
  • Apply risk-based thinking
  • Identify risks and opportunities
  • Align operations with QMS
  • Support continuous improvement

Who this is for

  • Employees across all departments
  • Supervisors and team leaders
  • Quality management representatives

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

ISO 9001 only works when people beyond the quality team understand the system. Shared awareness reduces resistance, improves process discipline, and supports stronger audit readiness.

Ideal as an organisation-wide foundation before deeper auditor or implementer training.
