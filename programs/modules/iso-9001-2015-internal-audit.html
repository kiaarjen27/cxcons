Practical ISO 9001 requirements and internal audit skills for quality, process, and compliance teams.

Program overview

ISO 9001:2015 & Internal Audit develops both system understanding and audit capability. Participants learn how to interpret requirements, apply risk-based auditing, plan effective audit programs, identify nonconformities and opportunities for improvement, and prepare professional audit reports.

Designed for internal auditors, QA/QC staff, and process owners supporting ISO implementation.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand ISO 9001 requirements
  • Apply risk-based auditing
  • Plan effective audit programs
  • Identify NC and OFI accurately
  • Prepare professional audit reports

Who this is for

  • Internal auditors and QA/QC staff
  • Engineers, supervisors, and process owners
  • Management representatives and ISO implementers

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Strong internal audit capability protects certification readiness and drives real process improvement. Teams that audit with clarity find issues earlier and close gaps more effectively.

Especially useful for manufacturers, service providers, and organisations maintaining ISO 9001 systems.
