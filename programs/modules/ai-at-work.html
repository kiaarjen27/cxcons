Program overview

AI at Work helps employees use artificial intelligence tools with confidence in real workplace tasks. Participants learn how to recognise practical AI opportunities, write clearer instructions, apply AI to daily work, and verify outputs before acting on them.

Designed for teams that want productivity gains without technical complexity — focused on responsible, useful application.

Learning outcomes

  • Recognise AI’s workplace potential
  • Write effective AI instructions
  • Apply AI to daily tasks
  • Verify AI-generated information
  • Use AI responsibly

Who this is for

  • Staff seeking practical AI workplace skills
  • Teams handling operations and communication tasks
  • Employees exploring productivity with AI tools

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

AI tools are already entering daily work. Without shared skills and guardrails, results are inconsistent and risk increases. This program builds practical capability and responsible use across teams.

Ideal for organisations introducing AI productivity habits without requiring technical specialists.
