Program overview

One Day Bullying & Harassment Prevention helps managers and professionals understand workplace harassment risks, recognise different forms of misconduct, and apply prevention and response strategies with legal awareness. The focus is on practical judgment and compliant handling.

Designed for leaders and professionals responsible for workplace culture, communication, and case escalation discipline.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand workplace sexual harassment
  • Identify harassment forms and challenges
  • Apply prevention and response strategies
  • Recognise employer and employee rights
  • Manage cases with legal compliance

Who this is for

  • Senior and line managers across departments
  • Supervisors handling workplace communication issues
  • Professionals seeking compliance and prevention skills

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Harassment and bullying risks affect trust, retention, and legal exposure. Clear awareness and response skills help organisations prevent harm and handle concerns more consistently.

A strong fit for management cohorts, HR reinforcement, and culture-compliance initiatives.
