Program overview

Finance for Non Finance helps executives and managers across departments understand financial language, interpret reports, and make stronger business decisions without needing a finance background. Ideal for HR, procurement, operations, and compliance professionals who work with budgets and commercial data.

Learning outcomes

  • Use financial data effectively
  • Analyse costs and expenses
  • Prepare budgets and projections
  • Read financial reports confidently
  • Understand financial terms and jargon

Prepared for

  • Executives and managers across departments
  • HR, procurement, and compliance professionals
  • Non-finance staff seeking financial literacy

Why this program matters

Non-finance leaders often influence cost, pricing, and resource decisions. This program builds the confidence to engage with finance teams, challenge numbers constructively, and align departmental plans with commercial reality.
