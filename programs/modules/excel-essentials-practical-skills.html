Program overview

This basic-to-intermediate Excel program helps staff work faster and more accurately in spreadsheets. Participants learn conditional formatting, lookup and text functions, effective worksheet auditing, and secure workbook protection.

Ideal for teams that already use Excel but need stronger practical skill and consistency.

Learning outcomes

  • Master conditional formatting
  • Apply lookup functions
  • Use text functions
  • Audit worksheets effectively
  • Protect workbooks securely

Who this is for

  • Department heads and managers
  • HR practitioners and line managers
  • Staff with basic or intermediate Excel knowledge
