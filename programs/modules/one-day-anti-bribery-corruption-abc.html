One Day Anti Bribery & Corruption (ABC)

Build practical awareness of bribery and corruption risks, corporate liability, and integrity expectations at work.

Program overview

This one-day ABC program helps participants understand bribery and corruption laws, recognise corporate liability provisions, apply adequate compliance measures, and identify proper reporting procedures. It also reinforces the value of integrity across the organisation.

Suitable for leadership, managers, employees, and external parties who interact with the business.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand bribery and corruption laws
  • Recognize corporate liability provisions
  • Apply adequate compliance measures
  • Identify corruption reporting procedures
  • Appreciate integrity in organizations

Who this is for

  • Board members and senior management
  • Managers, supervisors, and department heads
  • Employees, contractors, suppliers, and associates

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Anti-bribery awareness is strongest when people understand both legal exposure and day-to-day behavioural expectations. A clear one-day program supports compliance culture and risk reduction.

Useful for induction, annual compliance refreshers, and vendor-facing teams.
