Program overview

One Day Anti Bribery and Corruption (ABC) builds practical awareness of bribery risks, corporate liability, and integrity expectations at work. Participants learn how to recognise risk situations, apply compliance measures, and follow reporting procedures with greater confidence.

Suitable for board, management, employees, contractors, and associates who need a clear shared standard.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand bribery and corruption laws
  • Recognise corporate liability provisions
  • Apply adequate compliance measures
  • Identify corruption reporting procedures
  • Appreciate integrity in organisations

Who this is for

  • Board members and senior management
  • Managers, supervisors, and department heads
  • Employees, contractors, suppliers, and associates

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Integrity failures create legal, financial, and reputational risk. A clear one-day ABC program helps organisations set expectations and strengthen prevention awareness across stakeholders.

Useful for compliance programmes, induction reinforcement, and supplier/partner awareness sessions.
