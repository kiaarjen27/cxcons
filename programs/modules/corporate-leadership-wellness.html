Program overview

Corporate Leadership & Wellness connects leadership effectiveness with personal sustainability. Participants strengthen leadership behaviours while learning practical fitness, stress management, nutrition, and wellness habits that support long-term performance.

Designed for leaders who need both people-leadership skills and healthier operating habits under workplace pressure.

Learning outcomes

  • Demonstrate effective leadership skills
  • Apply practical fitness strategies
  • Utilize stress management techniques
  • Adopt balanced nutrition habits
  • Integrate holistic wellness practices

Who this is for

  • Management, supervisors, and team leaders
  • Executives across all organisational levels
  • Admin personnel and professionals

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Leadership performance is not only about tasks and targets. Leaders who manage energy, stress, and wellbeing are better positioned to lead consistently and support healthier team cultures.

A strong fit for organisations investing in leadership development with a practical wellness dimension.
