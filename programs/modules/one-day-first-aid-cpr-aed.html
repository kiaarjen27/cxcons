One Day First Aid, CPR and AED

A focused one-day program to strengthen workplace emergency response and first aid readiness.

Program overview

Designed for organisations that need practical emergency skills in a single day. Participants learn legal workplace context, CPR and AED application, and how to manage spinal, fracture, and wound situations with clearer response discipline.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand workplace legal provisions
  • Identify common causes of death
  • Apply CPR and AED correctly
  • Manage spinal, fracture, and wound care
  • Enhance workplace emergency preparedness

Prepared for

  • Safety officers and OSH practitioners
  • First aiders and emergency response teams
  • HR and security staff in organisations
