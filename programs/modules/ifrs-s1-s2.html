Program overview

This program helps participants apply IFRS S1 disclosures, implement IFRS S2 requirements, conduct materiality assessments, align with stakeholder expectations, and integrate ESG considerations into organisational strategy.

Designed for finance, sustainability, compliance, and leadership teams preparing for stronger disclosure practice.

Learning outcomes

  • Apply IFRS S1 disclosures
  • Implement IFRS S2 requirements
  • Conduct materiality assessments
  • Align with stakeholder expectations
  • Integrate ESG into strategy

Who this is for

  • Corporate leaders and finance executives
  • Sustainability and ESG professionals
  • Compliance officers and risk managers

Sustainability disclosure is moving from optional narrative to structured reporting expectation. Teams that understand IFRS S1 and S2 can prepare clearer, more decision-useful disclosures.

A timely module for listed companies, finance leaders, and ESG-facing functions.
