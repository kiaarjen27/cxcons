Program overview

One Day Combat Work Stress helps managers and professionals recognise workplace pressure early and respond with practical techniques. The focus is on usable skills — not theory — so participants leave with clearer ways to manage emotions, protect focus, and sustain performance.

Suitable for public runs or fully customised in-house delivery, and can be structured as an HRDC claimable program.

Learning outcomes

  • Recognise workplace stress factors
  • Identify burnout warning signs
  • Apply stress reduction techniques
  • Control emotions under pressure
  • Improve focus and productivity

Who this is for

  • Managers, supervisors, and team leaders
  • Executives across all levels
  • Admin staff and professionals

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Unmanaged stress affects decision quality, team climate, and attendance. A focused one-day format helps organisations equip people quickly with practical awareness and response tools.

Ideal when you need a clear, time-efficient intervention that supports resilience and day-to-day performance.
