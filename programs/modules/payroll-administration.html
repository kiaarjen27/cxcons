Program overview

Payroll Administration builds practical competence in managing payroll with greater accuracy and compliance awareness. Participants cover Malaysian employment law context, payroll components and deductions, overtime and leave pay calculations, benefits and incentives handling, and effective payroll audit practices.

Designed for HR, finance, and accounting staff responsible for reliable payroll operations.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand Malaysian employment laws
  • Master payroll components and deductions
  • Calculate overtime and leave pay
  • Manage employee benefits and incentives
  • Conduct payroll audits effectively

Who this is for

  • Payroll managers and administrators
  • HR and finance professionals
  • Accounting staff handling payroll

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Payroll errors create compliance risk, employee dissatisfaction, and rework. Stronger process knowledge improves accuracy and strengthens audit readiness.

Especially valuable for teams handling overtime, leave pay, benefits, and recurring payroll controls.
