Program overview

This program helps participants identify chemical hazards quickly, apply safe handling procedures, select the correct PPE, execute a spill response plan, and strengthen workplace compliance with OSHA and EQA requirements.

Designed for plant, engineering, and safety teams working with hazardous chemicals.

Learning outcomes

  • Identify chemical hazards quickly
  • Apply safe handling procedures
  • Select correct PPE effectively
  • Execute spill response plan
  • Ensure OSHA/EQA compliance

Who this is for

  • Chemical plant operators and technicians
  • Safety and Health Committee members
  • Engineers, supervisors, and OSH coordinators

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Chemical incidents escalate when teams hesitate on hazard recognition, PPE choice, or spill response. Structured training reduces operational risk and supports regulatory readiness.

A practical safety module for plants, labs, and industrial sites.
