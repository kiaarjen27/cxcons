Program overview

IATF 16949 Awareness and Internal Auditor builds practical understanding of automotive quality requirements and internal audit discipline. Participants learn risk-based thinking, core tool application, confident audit execution, and effective corrective action follow-through.

Designed for quality, compliance, and process improvement teams in automotive supply chains.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand IATF 16949 requirements
  • Apply risk-based thinking effectively
  • Use automotive core tools correctly
  • Conduct internal audits confidently
  • Implement corrective actions successfully

Who this is for

  • Quality managers and internal auditors
  • Automotive suppliers and manufacturers
  • Compliance and process improvement teams

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Automotive quality systems demand consistent interpretation and audit discipline. Strong internal capability reduces nonconformity risk and supports more reliable process control.

Especially valuable for suppliers strengthening audit readiness and internal quality ownership.
