Program overview

Mastering Professional Image Etiquette helps professionals present themselves with confidence and consistency. Participants learn how to refine personal style and grooming, dress appropriately for workplace success, apply practical colour awareness, and demonstrate stronger etiquette in professional interactions.

Designed for leaders and client-facing staff whose presence represents the organisation.

Learning outcomes

  • Present confidently and professionally
  • Identify personal style and grooming
  • Dress appropriately for workplace success
  • Apply colour analysis effectively
  • Demonstrate etiquette in interactions

Who this is for

  • Senior leaders representing organisational image
  • Executives and managers leading professional teams
  • Client-facing staff and young professionals

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Professional image shapes first impressions, client confidence, and internal standards. Clear etiquette and presence skills help teams represent the brand more consistently.

Especially useful for customer-facing roles, leadership cohorts, and high-visibility professional teams.
