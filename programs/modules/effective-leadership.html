Program overview

Effective Leadership helps mid-level managers and supervisors lead with clarity and credibility. Participants focus on trust-building, communication, fair decision-making, and reducing unhelpful micromanagement habits that limit team ownership.

Designed for leaders who need practical behaviours they can apply immediately with diverse teams.

Learning outcomes

  • Build trust through leadership
  • Communicate with clarity
  • Reduce micromanagement habits
  • Make fair workplace decisions
  • Empower teams effectively

Who this is for

  • Mid-level managers seeking leadership growth
  • Team leaders handling diverse staff
  • Supervisors aiming to inspire performance

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Technical competence alone does not create strong leadership. Teams perform better under leaders who communicate clearly, decide fairly, and give people room to own their work.

A practical foundation for supervisors and managers stepping into broader people-leadership responsibility.
