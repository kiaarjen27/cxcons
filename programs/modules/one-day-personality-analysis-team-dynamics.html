Program overview

This one-day program helps participants identify dominant personality styles, recognise behavioural communication cues, adapt their approach to different personalities, manage workplace personality clashes, and improve collaboration and teamwork.

Useful for leaders and teams that need better interpersonal effectiveness.

  • Recognize behavioural communication cues

  • Improve collaboration and teamwork
