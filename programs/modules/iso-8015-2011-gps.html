Program overview

This program covers GPS fundamentals, the benefits of geometric tolerancing, interpretation of design drawing requirements, setting of quality control criteria, and correct identification of datum features.

Designed for engineering, quality, manufacturing, and related technical teams.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand GPS fundamentals
  • Explain geometric tolerancing benefits
  • Interpret design drawing requirements
  • Set quality control criteria
  • Identify datum features correctly

Who this is for

  • Mechanical engineers and design drafters
  • Quality, inspection, and manufacturing staff
  • Procurement, tooling, and production teams

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Drawing misinterpretation drives scrap, rework, and supplier disputes. Shared GPS literacy improves communication between design, quality, and production.
