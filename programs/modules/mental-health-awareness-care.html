Program overview

Mental Health Awareness (C.A.R.E) helps teams strengthen focus, emotional regulation, and constructive responses under pressure. The program supports healthier workplace culture while improving day-to-day resilience and collaboration.

Suitable for executives, supervisors, and mixed employee groups across departments.

Learning outcomes

  • Improve focus and adaptability
  • Demonstrate emotional regulation skills
  • Apply coping strategies effectively
  • Resolve conflicts constructively
  • Strengthen workplace resilience

Who this is for

  • Executives and management teams
  • Frontline and supervisory staff
  • Professionals across all departments

HRDC claimable · Available as public or in-house training

Why organisations choose this program

Mental health capability is now a core workplace skill. Teams that can recognise pressure, regulate responses, and support one another perform more sustainably and communicate more constructively.

A strong fit for organisations investing in resilience, culture, and healthier performance environments.
