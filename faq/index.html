HRDC & claims

What is HRDC claimable training?

HRDC claimable training is structured learning that eligible Malaysian employers may claim under HRD Corp schemes, subject to current guidelines, documentation, attendance, and approval.

Are CXCons programs HRDC claimable?

Many CXCons programs are designed as HRDC claimable pathways. Final eligibility depends on employer status, scheme rules, attendance, and complete supporting documents. See also HRDCorp Hub.

What costs can be considered under HRDC claims?

Depending on the scheme and the current Allowable Cost Matrix (ACM), claimable items may include course fees and other approved cost components. Always verify against the latest HRD Corp guidelines.

Does CXCons help with HRDC claim documentation?

We support employers with program information and practical guidance so claims can be prepared more cleanly. Official approval remains with HRD Corp.

What is the difference between public and in-house HRDC training?

Public programs follow a published schedule with open seats. In-house programs are tailored for one organisation’s team, schedule, and objectives. Compare options on Programs.

Can in-house training still be HRDC claimable?

Yes, when requirements are met. Share your objectives and headcount so we can advise a suitable pathway. Start at Request custom programs.

What is the Allowable Cost Matrix (ACM)?

The ACM outlines cost categories and limits relevant to HRD Corp claims. CXCons helps employers interpret ACM implications for planning; it is not a substitute for official HRD Corp advice.

Do you provide guidance on common claim routes?

We help employers understand practical training and documentation steps related to common claim routes. Scheme names and rules can change — always check current HRD Corp circulars.

What documents are usually needed for smoother claims?

Typical items include attendance records, invoices, course outlines, and other evidence required by the chosen scheme. Exact requirements vary by case.
