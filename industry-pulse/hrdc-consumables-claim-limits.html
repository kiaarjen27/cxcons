Training Consumables: Where Claims Get Messy

Materials costs look small until the file cannot explain them. Itemisation and evidence matter.

If it cannot be explained, it is hard to defend

Bundled “training kit” lines without clarity create queries. Keep quotations and invoices readable so a reviewer can see what was bought and why.

Practical controls

  • Itemise materials when amounts are material
  • Keep supplier quotations with the claim pack
  • Separate materials from professional fees clearly
  • Avoid last-minute cash purchases with no trail

Do not wait for a query

If materials are part of the design, document them during procurement — not after finance asks what “misc training costs” means.

Ask for clearer commercial structure up front
