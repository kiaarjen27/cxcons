Flexible Growth: E-Learning Claims Standardized

E-learning is no longer a stopgap measure; it is a permanent fixture in modern corporate training. The latest ACM guidelines have standardized the claiming process for e-learning, making it highly predictable for employers.

HRD Corp now supports e-learning claims based on training hours, with a set rate of RM125 per hour per pax. This transparent structure allows for easy calculation—for example, a 7-hour course nets a maximum financial assistance of RM875 per pax.

Employers should note that the training duration must be at least one hour and include at least one complete module to be eligible. The claiming process is now more efficient, with clear indicators on the e-TRiS platform to input training hours, ensuring that your company can scale its learning initiatives without administrative bottlenecks.
