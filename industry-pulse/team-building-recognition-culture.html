Team Building and Recognition Culture

Recognition that only happens on stage feels fake. Peer recognition habits can be practised in facilitated sessions.

Practise the muscle

Structured appreciation rounds and peer call-outs can transfer if managers continue them after the event.

Design tips

  • Specific behaviour recognition, not generic praise
  • Peer-to-peer formats
  • Manager modelling
  • Link to values already in the handbook

Avoid empty awards

If recognition is disconnected from real work standards, staff treat it as theatre. Specificity is the difference.
