Safety Leadership: It’s Not Just for the OSH Officer

The OSH Act 1994, as updated in 2024, shifts the responsibility for safety from a single individual to every level of management. If you are a department head, safety is now a core performance indicator.

Section 15 of the Act makes it the duty of every employer to ensure safety and health at work. For non-HR managers, this means actively implementing risk assessments and emergency procedures. The 2024 amendments have dramatically increased fines, making non-compliance a significant financial risk for any business unit.
