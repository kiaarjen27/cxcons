Set an In-House Minimum Headcount Policy

In-house training below a viable cohort often fails commercially and administratively.

Policy beats case-by-case argument

Publish a minimum headcount and exception route so managers plan recruitment of learners earlier — instead of lobbying after the trainer is booked.

Policy elements

  • Minimum confirmed seats by programme type
  • Cutoff date for go / no-go
  • Option to convert to public seats
  • Exception approval owner

Why this protects claim quality

Thin cohorts change cost assumptions and attendance risk. A clear policy reduces forced runs that produce weak files.
