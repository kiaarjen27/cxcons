Practical Mastery: Utilizing the On-the-Job Training (OJT) Scheme

On-the-Job Training (OJT) is essential for bridging the gap between theory and practice. This scheme allows skilled supervisors to pass on technical knowledge directly to new hires on the factory floor or in the office.

The OJT scheme supports a trainer-to-trainee ratio of 1:1 up to 1:4, making it highly effective for targeted mentorship. Internal trainers can be compensated at RM50 per trainee per hour, providing an incentive for your best workers to mentor new recruits.

Unlike other grants, OJT applications can be submitted after the training session, provided it is within 6 months of the training completion date. This allows for flexibility in capturing the informal yet critical transfer of skills that occurs daily in your organization, from technical certifications to professional assessments under the Malaysian Board of Technologists.
