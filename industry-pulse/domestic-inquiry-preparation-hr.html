Domestic Inquiry Preparation: HR Checklist

DI quality is mostly preparation quality. Weak notice and scattered evidence undermine the process before the hearing starts.

Process credibility is built before the room is booked

A domestic inquiry is judged on fairness of process as much as on the eventual finding. Rushed allegation letters, incomplete bundles, and interested panel members create avoidable challenges.

Preparation essentials

  • Clear allegation framing — specific conduct, dates, policy/rule referenced
  • Document bundle organised chronologically (not a dump of emails)
  • Notice timelines respected so the employee can prepare a reply
  • Panel impartiality checked (no direct accuser as decision-maker)
  • Hearing notes standard agreed in advance

Tone

Firm and fair beats aggressive theatre. The goal is a defensible record of inquiry — not a performance of anger.

Same-week checklist

  • Allegation letter drafted and reviewed
  • Bundle indexed
  • Panel confirmed available and independent
  • Room / virtual logistics and recording rules set
  • HR script for opening the hearing agreed
