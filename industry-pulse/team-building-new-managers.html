Team Building When a New Manager Takes Over

A new manager inherits history. A facilitated team session can accelerate trust and clarify working norms before small frictions harden.

Session goals that help

  • Surface expectations safely
  • Clarify decision rights
  • Practice feedback language
  • Build early wins as a unit

Tone

Do not force loyalty theatre. Focus on how the team will work together under the new leadership reality.

Follow-through

Agree two team norms and review them after two weeks.

Support path

Combine a team session with supervisory skill modules if the manager is new to people leadership.
