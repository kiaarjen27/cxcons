When to Contact CXCons Directly

Self-serve for search. Talk to us for multi-site, sensitive, or complex academy-style needs. The website is built so you can explore modules and request brochures without waiting — direct contact is for when the brief will not fit a single form field.

Industry Pulse · Working with CXCons

Program Finder, module pages, TNA, and brochure requests cover most straightforward needs. Contacting the team early saves time when coordination, confidentiality, or compliance pressure is part of the job.

Self-serve is enough when

  • You are exploring titles and want outlines
  • One module or a short shortlist for one team
  • Standard in-house or public-style interest, no special constraints
  • You already know the theme and mainly need timing and brochure detail

Contact CXCons directly when

  • Multi-site rollouts — same theme across locations, staggered dates, or mixed languages
  • Sensitive teams — post-restructure, conflict, or confidential leadership work
  • Tight compliance timelines — audit, regulator, or board-driven deadlines
  • Complex academies — multi-module pathways, blended coaching + workshops, phased cohorts
  • Events + learning in one brief — team building, CSR, or conference-style delivery with training outcomes

What to bring to the conversation

  • Audience and approximate headcount
  • Locations and preferred window
  • Outcome that matters (skill, compliance, culture, or event experience)
  • Constraints (budget band, HRDC, language, confidentiality)

Bottom line

Use the site to search and request. Contact CXCons directly when the work is multi-site, sensitive, compliance-heavy, or too layered for a single brochure form — that is when a short conversation prevents the wrong proposal.
