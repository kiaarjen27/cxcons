Employee Monitoring Boundaries

Monitoring without purpose limitation creates trust damage and regulatory exposure.

Purpose, notice, proportionality

If you monitor, employees should understand what, why, and who can access the data. Silent expansion of tools is how trust collapses.

Governance questions

  • What business risk justifies monitoring?
  • Have employees been informed in plain language?
  • Who can access raw data?
  • How long is data retained?
