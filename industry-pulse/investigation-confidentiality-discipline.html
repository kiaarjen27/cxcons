Investigation Confidentiality Discipline

Corridor commentary can contaminate witnesses and harm reputations before facts are tested.

Need-to-know is a rule, not a mood

Managers must not narrate ongoing investigations to bystanders. Curiosity is not a business need.

Controls

  • Remind parties of confidentiality expectations
  • Limit document access
  • Train managers on what they may say
  • Address breaches seriously

Why leaks destroy process

Witnesses change stories under social pressure. Respondents face trial by gossip. Complainants lose trust in the system.
