Sexual Harassment Policy: From Shelf to Practice

Policies fail in the first manager response. Awkward handling can retraumatise and destroy trust.

Train the first response

Managers need a simple script: listen, do not investigate alone if policy says escalate, protect confidentiality, document factually.

Keep the policy alive

  • Onboarding awareness — not a PDF buried in a portal
  • Periodic refreshers with scenarios
  • Visible reporting routes
  • Leadership consistency in enforcement

What consistency looks like

Similar cases receive similar process seriousness regardless of the respondent’s rank. Selective seriousness teaches silence.
