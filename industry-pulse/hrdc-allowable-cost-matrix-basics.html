HRDC Allowable Cost Matrix: What Employers Should Know First

The Allowable Cost Matrix shapes what can reasonably sit inside an HRD Corp claim. Employers who plan training without reading ACM logic often discover gaps only at claim stage.

What the ACM is for

It sets out cost categories and limits linked to claimable training. Your program design, venue, meals, and related costs should be checked against current ACM guidance before you commit spend.

Common planning mistakes

  • Assuming every training-related cost is claimable
  • Leaving documentation to the end of the program
  • Mixing leisure-heavy activity with weak learning objectives
  • Using outdated ACM assumptions after policy updates

Practical habit for HR and L&D

Before confirming a public or in-house run, list the intended cost lines, match them to ACM categories, and keep the outline, attendance, and invoices in one claim file from day one.
