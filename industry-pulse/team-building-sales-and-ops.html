Sales and Operations Team Building

If handoffs fail, a karaoke night will not fix revenue leakage. Design for process empathy.

Shared pain points beat generic games

Map the friction first: lead quality, delivery promises, escalations, credit notes, and the language each side uses about the other. Then design activities that force perspective-taking on those exact joints.

Design focus that transfers

  • Walk a real order lifecycle together (anonymised cases)
  • Role-swap understanding exercises under time pressure
  • Draft joint service-level agreements the teams can test for 30 days
  • Debrief on language that triggers conflict (“you always…”, “sales promised…”)

Success signals after 30 days

  • Clearer written handoffs
  • Fewer surprise escalations
  • Joint problem ownership on recurring failure modes
