Handling Sensitive Employee Data: What HR Needs to Know

The PDPA classifies health records, political opinions, and religious beliefs as "sensitive personal data." For HR, this means a much higher standard of protection is required under Section 40.

Processing sensitive data requires explicit consent from the employee. Exceptions apply only for specific needs, such as medical purposes performed by healthcare professionals or legal proceedings.

HR teams must distinguish between general data and sensitive data to avoid accidental breaches.
