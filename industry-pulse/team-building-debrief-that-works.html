Team Building Debrief That Actually Works

Activities create shared experience. Debrief turns experience into agreement. Without it, teams remember noise and forget behaviour change.

Debrief questions that transfer

  • What happened under pressure?
  • What helped the team succeed?
  • What showed up that also shows up at work?
  • What will we do differently next week?

Facilitation habits

Ask, wait, write visible commitments, and assign owners where relevant.

Manager role after the day

Review the two commitments in the next team meeting.

Our team building designs treat debrief as core, not optional filler.
