Online Learning Rules Under HRDC: ROT & E-Learning

Delivering a course over Zoom or Teams does not mean copying a physical training grant application and changing the venue to “online.” HRD Corp governs Remote Online Training (ROT) and e-learning under distinct fee ceilings, minimum hour rules, and digital attendance standards.

Industry Pulse · HRD Corp compliance & online delivery

Online training is convenient for multi-branch teams, but employers often lose claim value when they price online sessions at physical classroom ceilings or rely on manual sign-in sheets. Understanding how HRD Corp separates live Remote Online Training (ROT) from asynchronous e-learning and hybrid delivery keeps grant budgets accurate from day one.

Core rules for Remote Online Training (ROT)

  • Minimum 4-hour training duration — standard ROT programmes require at least 4 training hours, though hours can be accumulated across scheduled sessions to hit the minimum
  • Distinct ROT fee ceilings — under the Allowable Cost Matrix, In-House ROT caps at RM10,500/group/day (7 hours) or RM6,000/group/half-day (4 hours), while Public ROT caps at RM1,750/pax/day or RM1,000/pax/half-day (inclusive of SST)
  • System-generated attendance is mandatory — physical sign-in sheets do not replace platform logs; claims require system-generated attendance reports (such as Zoom, Teams, or LMS timestamp logs) showing participant join/leave durations
  • Internet data allowance (In-House ROT only) — employers may claim up to RM100 per group for internet data costs on In-House ROT programmes without needing supporting receipts
  • Hybrid programmes must be declared upfront — if a programme combines physical classroom days and ROT sessions, the training schedule must explicitly state the delivery mode for every session block

2026 grant lead times and zero-amendment risks

Under HRD Corp’s updated 2026 grant procedures (Employer’s Circular No. 2/2026), approved grants are locked — employers can no longer modify dates, trainers, or switch an approved physical class into an ROT session after approval. If an instructor falls ill or a travel disruption forces a move online, the approved grant must be cancelled and a fresh application submitted within allowable lead-time windows.

Practical operating habits

  • Confirm whether delivery is live ROT, self-paced e-learning, or hybrid before requesting a quotation from the provider
  • Require trainers to export raw platform attendance logs (showing full participant names and login durations) immediately after each online module
  • Brief participants to log in using their full NRIC-matching names rather than nicknames or shared conference-room accounts
  • Ensure any licensed digital learning materials are pre-approved by HRD Corp and attached at the grant application stage
  • Submit grant applications early with confirmed dates so there is zero pressure to switch delivery modes last minute

What to do next

Establish one ROT fee-ceiling check, one participant login naming rule, and one same-day platform log export habit for all virtual classes. Clean digital attendance trails and accurate cost ceilings protect every online grant from post-training queries.

Bottom line

Online training lowers travel and venue overhead, but HRD Corp audits digital delivery through strict hour logic, lower ROT ceilings, and verifiable system logs. Locking the delivery format and digital attendance workflow before class starts keeps online claims smooth from approval to payout.
