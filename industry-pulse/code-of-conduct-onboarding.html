Code of Conduct Belongs in Onboarding Week

New hires absorb culture early. Delayed ethics briefing concedes the informal culture already forming.

Day-one signals matter

What you emphasise in week one becomes “what counts here.” If ethics appears only in month six, informal norms win first.

Make it practical

  • Scenarios over slogan slides
  • Reporting channels explained
  • Gifts / conflicts examples
  • Manager reinforcement in the first month

Acknowledgement is not understanding

An e-sign proves receipt. Behaviour change needs discussion of grey zones people will actually face.
