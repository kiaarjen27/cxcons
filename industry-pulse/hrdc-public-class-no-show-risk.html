Public Class No-Shows Waste More Than a Seat

A paid seat with no learner is a planning failure. No-shows also weaken the internal case for future training investment.

Nomination is a management act

When managers nominate casually, no-shows rise. Treat nomination as commitment against a named gap — not a favour or a free day out of the office.

Reduce no-show risk

  • Require a one-line learning purpose with every nomination
  • Confirm attendance 48 hours before class
  • Keep a standby nominee where policy allows
  • Review repeat no-show patterns by department — not only individual excuses

After a no-show

Record it. Patterns matter more than one incident. If the same unit burns seats quarterly, fix the nomination process before buying more seats.

Shortlist public and in-house options deliberately

Use Program Finder and programme pathways when seats must be intentional.
