Feedback Culture: Say It Early, Write It Clearly

Many workplace conflicts grow because feedback arrives too late. Managers who speak early and document fairly reduce formal disputes and improve performance rhythm.

What weak feedback looks like

  • Silence until appraisal season
  • Public criticism without private clarity
  • No written follow-up after verbal warnings
  • Praise with no standard, correction with no example

A simple manager routine

Notice the behaviour, describe the impact, agree the next standard, and note the conversation date. Consistency matters more than perfect wording.

Link to formal process

When issues continue, clean informal records make performance management or disciplinary steps clearer and fairer.

Build the skill

Communication and supervisory programs help managers practise feedback without turning every talk into a confrontation.
