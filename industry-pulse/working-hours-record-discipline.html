Working Hours Record Discipline

If hours are disputed, weak records become the employer’s problem.

Systems need supervisory enforcement

A biometric device does not fix buddy-clocking or offline manual overrides without review. Controls fail in the exception report that nobody reads.

Controls

  • Review exception reports weekly
  • Restrict manual overrides to named roles
  • Reconcile overtime approvals to records
  • Address patterns, not only single anomalies

Why it escalates

Disputes over hours rarely stay “about one shift.” They become stories about fairness — especially when one team’s overrides are tolerated and another’s are not.
