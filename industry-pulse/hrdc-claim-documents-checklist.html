HRDC Claim Documents: A Practical Employer Checklist

Strong training delivery can still fail at claim stage when documents are incomplete. Treat evidence as part of program design, not an afterthought.

Documents employers should plan for

  • Approved training grant / relevant scheme references where applicable
  • Program outline and schedule
  • Attendance records with required thresholds in mind
  • Invoices and proof of payment aligned to claim items
  • Trainer details and supporting program materials

Process tip

Create a claim folder before the training day. Save the outline, attendance sheet template, and vendor invoice path in advance so nothing is reconstructed later under pressure.

Public vs in-house

Documentation discipline matters for both. In-house runs often need tighter internal records because delivery variables are controlled by the employer and provider together.
