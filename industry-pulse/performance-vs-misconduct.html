Poor Performance Is Not Automatically Misconduct

Managers often blend performance and misconduct into one emotional escalation. Separating the two protects fairness, documentation quality, and industrial outcomes.

Quick distinction

Performance is about capability or output against expectations. Misconduct is about behaviour that breaches rules, integrity, or lawful instructions.

Why the mix-up is costly

  • Wrong process path from day one
  • Employees unclear what they must correct
  • Disciplinary action that looks predetermined
  • Missed chance to coach before punishing

Manager checklist

Write the facts, name the standard that was missed, decide whether the issue is skill, will, or rule-breaking, then choose coaching, performance management, or disciplinary process accordingly.

Build capability

Supervisory programs on feedback, domestic inquiry basics, and Employment Act awareness reduce these errors across the management line.
