Set Team Building Objectives Before You Pick the Venue

Venue catalogues are exciting. Outcomes are clearer. Employers get better days when they define the behavioural target before locking a location.

Start with the Monday question

What should this team do better when they return to work? Collaboration under pressure, clearer communication, or rebuilt trust after change are usable targets.

Then choose format

  • Indoor corporate for tight schedules and formal debrief
  • Outdoor or adventure when energy and shared challenge matter
  • Hybrid days when both reflection and movement are needed

Avoid the common trap

Booking a beautiful venue first and reverse-engineering an objective later usually produces activity without transfer.

CXCons approach

State your outcome in the team building request form so facilitation and venue suggestions stay aligned.
