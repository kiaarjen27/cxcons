Remote Online Training Without Claim Headaches

Online delivery is claimable — but only when attendance, records, and platform evidence meet HRD Corp expectations.

ROT is not “just a Zoom session”

Under HRD Corp practice, Remote Online Training (ROT) is treated in line with face-to-face structures for many claim rules. That helps employers, but it also means online programmes still need disciplined attendance, duration proof, and proper claim documents — not only a meeting link.

1. What counts as Remote Online Training

  • Trainer delivers live, in real time, to trainees who are not in the same physical classroom
  • Delivery uses a virtual classroom / approved online platform
  • In grant applications, training location should clearly state Remote Online Training
  • E-learning with measurable hours/modules follows ACM e-learning hour logic
  • Minimum e-learning duration is generally 1 hour with at least one complete module

2. Attendance is still the claim gate

  • Complete at least 75% of total training hours
  • Allowances are prorated by attendance completion
  • Half-day logic still centres on the 4-hour threshold where applicable
  • For provider-submitted claims, system-generated attendance must be kept

For online claims, system-generated attendance should be signed and declared by both the training provider and the employer — with company stamp, authorised officer name/position, and date.

3. Records employers and providers should retain

  • Course content / module outline
  • Invoice or quotation aligned to the approved grant
  • Trainer profile (especially for mobile and remote online delivery)
  • Attendance T3 form where required
  • JD14 (Joint Declaration) where the claim route requires it
  • System-generated attendance logs for online sessions
  • Video records of live online sessions — keep for at least 2 years under online training guideline practice

4. Delivery quality rules that affect approval risk

  • Choose platforms that support real facilitation, not passive one-way streaming only
  • Training effectiveness evaluation remains compulsory for live online delivery
  • Trainees must be identifiable in attendance evidence
  • Do not treat incomplete self-paced clicks as full attendance without module completion proof
  • Where scheme rules differ (Grant vs Strategic Initiative), apply the correct attendance threshold for that scheme

5. Practical employer checklist before claim

  • Confirm ROT was selected correctly in the grant application
  • Match claimed hours to actual completed module time
  • Export and sign system attendance before claim submission
  • Keep recording links/files stored and retrievable for 2 years
  • Align invoice party (HRD Corp vs employer) with the scheme used
  • Check whether meal vs trainee allowance rules apply for the location/mode
