Modernize Your Training: The ALAT Scheme

Building a dedicated learning space is now more accessible than ever. The enhanced ALAT scheme allows you to utilize your HRDC levy to renovate training rooms and purchase essential training equipment.

You can now claim up to 50% of your levy balance for facility upgrades, including projector wiring, climate control, and digital connectivity. The best part? The "approved list" for equipment is now wider, giving you flexibility to acquire tools relevant to your specific industry nature.

At CXCONS, we guide you through the grant application process—from training room layouts to equipment justification—ensuring your infrastructure investments are fully compliant with the latest HRD Corp guidebook requirements.
