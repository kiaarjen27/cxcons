HRD Corp Claimable Courses (HCC)

HRD Corp Claimable Courses (HCC) is the main scheme that allows employers to retrain and upgrade their workforce with approved course fees supported through levy contributions. It is the operational backbone of most employer-funded training under HRD Corp — not a side programme.

Industry Pulse · HRD Corp

HCC is how many Malaysian employers convert levy into structured capability building. Used well, it funds in-house runs, public seats, online delivery, and development formats under one claims architecture. Used late or incompletely, it becomes a scramble of missed windows and weak grant files.

What HCC can cover

Employers can apply under HCC for a range of delivery modes, including:

  • In-house training
  • Public programmes
  • Remote online training
  • Development programmes
  • E-learning
  • Coaching and mentoring

Category, rate, and document rules still follow the current Allowable Cost Matrix and scheme instructions — HCC is the door; ACM and attendance rules still shape the amount.

Application timing

Applications must be submitted at least one day before training begins. In practice, strong teams submit earlier: trainer confirmation, headcount, and outline should be stable before the grant is filed — not patched the evening before day one.

Trainer accreditation (from January 2025)

Since January 2025, trainers must hold HRD Corp Accreditation status to deliver training under the claimable pathway. Employers should verify accreditation when shortlisting providers — not after the invoice arrives.

Upfront payment option

Employers may request up to 30% upfront payment during grant application, subject to refund if training is cancelled. Treat this as cash-flow support with conditions, not automatic entitlement: cancellation and documentation discipline still apply.

What still decides a clean claim

  • Grant submitted on time with consistent programme details
  • Attendance and completion evidence that matches the approved hours
  • Supporting documents aligned to ACM and scheme type
  • Title consistency across outline, attendance, and financial documents

Bottom line

HCC is the primary route for levy-backed workforce upgrading. Plan the mode, confirm accredited trainers, file before the start date, and keep attendance and documents tight — so the scheme funds development instead of generating rework.
