HRD Corp Claimable Courses (HCC)

HRD Corp Claimable Courses (HCC) is the main scheme that allows employers to retrain and upgrade their workforce without upfront payments. Approved course fees are debited directly from levy contributions.

Employers can apply for in-house, public, remote online, development programmes, e-learning, coaching, and mentoring. Applications must be submitted at least one day before training begins.

Since January 2025, trainers must hold HRD Corp Accreditation status to deliver training. Employers may also request up to 30% upfront payment during grant application, subject to refund if training is cancelled.
