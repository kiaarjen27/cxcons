Structuring Team Building with HRDC in Mind

When employers want team building to sit cleanly within a learning plan, structure matters: objectives, duration, facilitation, attendance, and debrief — not leisure only.

Structure checklist

  • Written learning objectives
  • Facilitated segments with debrief
  • Attendance discipline
  • Outline retained with HR records
  • Clear link to workplace behaviours

Honest boundary

Pure recreation with no learning design is a weak training story. Design the day as development.
