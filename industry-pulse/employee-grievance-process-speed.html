Grievance Process Speed Protects Trust

Silence during investigation feels like dismissal of the concern — even when HR is working.

Communicate process, not only outcome

Acknowledge receipt, explain steps, and give realistic timelines. People can tolerate hard outcomes better than unexplained silence.

Speed without recklessness

  • Acknowledge quickly
  • Separate urgent safety issues from longer fact-finding
  • Keep parties updated on stage, not gossip
  • Document decisions clearly

The trust equation

Fair outcome + long silence often still equals lost trust. Fair outcome + clear process communication preserves working relationships more often.
