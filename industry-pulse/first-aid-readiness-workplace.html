First Aid Readiness Is Response Confidence

A first aid box on the wall is not readiness. Readiness means enough trained people, clear escalation, and the confidence to act in the first minutes of an incident.

Common gaps

  • Certificates expired with no refresher plan
  • Trained staff concentrated on one shift only
  • No drill or scenario practice after training
  • Unclear who calls emergency services

What good readiness includes

Coverage across shifts, visible responders, maintained equipment, and periodic practice so skills do not fade.

Training options

One-day first aid, CPR and AED programs are a practical baseline for offices and operational sites. Match depth to workplace risk.
