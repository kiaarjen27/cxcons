Enhancements to Allowable Cost Matrix

HRD Corp’s 2024–2025 updates to the Allowable Cost Matrix (ACM) expanded claimable rates, allowances, and training categories to better support employers and industries.

Course fee limits increased: in-house training now allows up to RM10,500/day/group, while public training permits RM1,750/day/pax. E-learning claims are calculated hourly at RM125/pax.

Allowances were also raised: trainee/trainer allowance up to RM500/day for distances ≥100km, meal allowance up to RM100/day, and overseas allowance up to RM1,500/day/pax. These changes took effect November 2024, with hourly claims implemented in January 2025.
