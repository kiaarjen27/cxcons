Bullying and Harassment: Prevention Is a Management Habit

Policies help only when managers recognise early signals and respond consistently. Prevention training sets the standard before formal complaints become the first conversation.

Early signals teams ignore

  • Repeated public put-downs framed as “jokes”
  • Exclusion from information needed to do the job
  • Targeted over-monitoring of one person
  • Retaliation after a concern is raised

Manager response basics

Take reports seriously, separate facts from rumour, protect against retaliation, and involve HR when thresholds are crossed.

Why training matters

People need examples, not only definitions. Scenario-based sessions help staff test what is acceptable before real harm occurs.
