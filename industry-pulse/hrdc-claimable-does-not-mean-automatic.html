‘Claimable’ Is Not a Guarantee of Payment

Marketing language can say claimable. The claim file still has to earn approval under current rules.

Treat claimable as a design standard

It means the programme can be structured for a claim pathway — not that every invoice line will be reimbursed in full without conditions. Employers who hear “claimable” as “already paid” under-prepare attendance, hour logic, and supporting documents.

What still sits with the employer

  • Correct scheme / delivery assumptions (public vs in-house, trainer type)
  • Attendance and completion logic
  • Document completeness and internal handoff speed
  • Alignment between what was sold, delivered, and submitted

Practical buying questions

  • What evidence will we hold on the last training day?
  • Who is the claim owner inside our company?
  • Which cost lines are assumed claimable — and which are company cost?
  • What happens if headcount drops below the planned group size?
