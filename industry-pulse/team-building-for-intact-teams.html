Team Building for Intact Work Teams

Intact teams already share context. The opportunity is to repair friction, reset norms, and practice cooperation on tasks that resemble real work pressure.

What intact teams often need

  • Clearer role boundaries
  • Faster conflict recovery
  • Shared problem-solving under time pressure
  • Trust after missed handovers

Design implication

Use scenarios close to the team’s actual workflow. Generic games alone rarely change Monday behaviour.

Facilitation matters

Debrief is where intact teams connect the activity to agreements they will keep.
