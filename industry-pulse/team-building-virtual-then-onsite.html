Virtual First, Then On-Site Team Building

Sometimes a short virtual session clarifies objectives and norms before travel spend.

Sequence can reduce waste

Align expectations online, then use on-site time for higher-trust work that needs presence.

Virtual session uses

  • Objective setting
  • Norm drafting
  • Break ice across locations
  • Identify sensitive issues needing careful on-site design

When this helps most

Multi-site teams, mixed seniority, or unclear sponsors who need alignment before a large budget day.
