Flexible Working Policy Clarity for Managers

Hybrid and flexible arrangements need written norms, not personality-based exceptions.

Ambiguity creates perceived unfairness

Two employees with different informal deals will eventually compare notes. The organisation then faces a trust problem that looks like a “policy problem.”

Clarify

  • Eligibility and approval route
  • Core collaboration hours
  • Response-time expectations
  • How output is evidenced

Manager trap

Approving flexibility for high performers only, while denying it without reasons for others, teaches that rules are optional for favourites.

Align people practice with capability building

Supervisory and communication programmes help managers hold standards consistently.
