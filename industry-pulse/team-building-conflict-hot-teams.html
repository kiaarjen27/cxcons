Team Building for Conflict-Heavy Teams

Not every conflict belongs in a team-building format. Misuse can worsen harm.

Triage first

Harassment, violence risk, or formal grievances need proper channels before group activities. Putting unresolved formal issues into a “fun day” is not bravery — it is poor risk management.

When team building can help

  • Work-process conflict
  • Communication style clashes
  • Post-restructure suspicion without formal complaints

When to pause

  • Active formal investigations
  • Safety threats
  • Leaders unwilling to hear feedback
