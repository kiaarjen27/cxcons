OSH Duty of Care Is a Manager Habit

Safety posters do not supervise work. Managers do — in ordinary decisions under production pressure.

Duty of care shows up in ordinary decisions

Rushing a job without controls, ignoring near-misses, or mocking PPE compliance are duty failures in practice — even when the safety policy document is excellent.

Daily manager behaviours that count

  • Brief hazards before high-risk tasks — not only at annual training
  • Enforce stop-work authority seriously when it is used
  • Respond to reports without humiliation
  • Do not publicly reward speed that skipped controls

Near-miss posture

Calling a near-miss “lucky” teaches silence. Calling it data teaches prevention. Thank the reporter, capture facts, assign an owner and date, share learning without naming-and-shaming.
