Multiple Sessions Need One Claim Story

Split sessions create split evidence. Without a single narrative, claim files become puzzles.

One programme, many days — still one file logic

Multi-day programmes need consistent naming, attendance continuity, and invoice alignment across dates. Reviewers should not have to reverse-engineer whether Day 1 and Day 2 were the same intervention.

Keep coherence

  • Use one programme title consistently across all documents
  • Label attendance sheets by day and total hours
  • Ensure invoice references map to the full run
  • Assign one owner for the combined folder

Practical naming example

Prefer “Employment Act Workshop — 12–13 Nov 2026” on outline, attendance, and invoice notes — not three different short titles for the same event.
