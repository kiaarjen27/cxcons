Standardise Your Attendance Template

Every provider using a different sheet format creates avoidable cleanup work for HR and finance.

One internal standard, many providers

Where possible, require a minimum field set even if the provider uses their own form layout. Consistency is what makes monthly claim packing realistic.

Minimum fields

  • Programme title and date
  • Participant full name (legible)
  • Signature or approved electronic equivalent
  • Session hours
  • Employer and provider confirmation where required

Operational rule

Scan or export the same day. Do not let original sheets live in a trainer’s bag for a week.
