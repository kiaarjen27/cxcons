Multi-Day Team Retreat Design

Without an arc, day two collapses into fatigue and disengagement.

Arc over activity pile

Sequence relationship, productive conflict, joint work, and recovery. Stacking high-intensity games for three days is not “premium” — it is poor energy management.

Arc example

  • Day 1: climate and trust
  • Day 2: real business problem work
  • Day 3: commitments and close

Protect transfer

End with named owners and a 30-day review date. Retreats without follow-up become expensive memory and little else.
