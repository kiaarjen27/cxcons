OSH Duty of Care: What Leadership Cannot Delegate Away

Safety responsibility sits with how work is organised, not only with the safety officer. Malaysian employers need visible systems, competent people, and training that matches real risk.

Duty of care in plain terms

Leaders must take reasonable steps to protect people affected by the work — including employees and, in relevant situations, contractors working under direction.

Where organisations slip

  • Safety treated as paperwork only
  • Supervisors untrained for site realities
  • Contractors assumed to be fully outside responsibility
  • No refreshers after incidents or process changes

Training response that makes sense

Pair policy briefings with practical modules: first aid readiness, chemical handling, supervisory safety leadership, and incident response habits.
