Team Building Budget Bands: Be Honest Early

Vague budgets create mismatched proposals and slow decisions.

Range enables design

A realistic per-pax or total range lets partners choose venue class, facilitation depth, and F&B correctly. “Competitive please” forces guesswork and polite but useless proposals.

Share early

  • Headcount range
  • Indoor / outdoor preference
  • Need for overnight or transport
  • Primary outcome
  • Budget band (even a low–high range is enough)

What honesty buys you

Faster shortlists, fewer rejected quotes, and designs that match what leadership will actually approve.
