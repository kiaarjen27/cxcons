The Chain of Responsibility: Duties of Principals

The 2024 amendments to the Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 clarify that safety responsibility is not limited to direct employees. Section 18A introduces a duty of care for principals toward contractors — companies cannot outsource safety liability by hiring third parties alone.

A principal is anyone who contracts with another to carry out work for their business. Under Section 18A, a principal must ensure the safety and health of any contractor, subcontractor, or their employees, when those workers operate under the principal’s direction.

What this requires in practice

  • Allocate sufficient time, budget, and resources for safe systems of work
  • Treat external vendors under your direction as part of your safety accountability chain
  • Do not assume the contractor’s own OSH file removes your duty

Penalties

Failure to fulfil these duties is a serious offence under Section 19, carrying a penalty of up to RM500,000 or two years’ imprisonment.

Bottom line

If you direct the work, you share responsibility for safe conditions. Section 18A is designed so safety cannot be contracted away on paper while control stays with the principal.
