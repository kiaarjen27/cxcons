Measuring Team Building Impact Simply

You do not need a complex ROI model. You need a few observable signals tied to the original objective, reviewed within 30 days.

Lightweight signals

  • Fewer handover complaints
  • Faster issue escalation
  • More cross-helping on busy days
  • Manager observation notes on target behaviours

Set this before the day

If you cannot name the signal before the event, measurement after will be vague.
