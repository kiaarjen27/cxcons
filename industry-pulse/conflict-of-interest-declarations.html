Conflict of Interest Declarations Managers Understand

COI risk appears in hiring, procurement, and vendor relationships — not only boardrooms.

Make examples concrete

Staff understand “do not approve your relative’s invoice” faster than abstract policy language. Training that only recites definitions rarely changes behaviour at the approval desk.

Common workplace zones

  • Hiring referrals and interview panels
  • Vendor selection and scorecards
  • Gifts and hospitality during tenders
  • Secondary employment that competes with the employer’s interests

Manager action

When in doubt, declare and step aside from the decision. Late disclosure after a problem appears is much harder to defend than early awkward honesty.
