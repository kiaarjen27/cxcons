Ethics and Integrity Team Sessions

Integrity culture is practised in grey-zone scenarios, not slogan banners.

Scenarios create transfer

Teams rehearse what they would do when a vendor offers hospitality or a colleague asks to bypass a control.

Scenario zones

  • Gifts and hospitality
  • Conflict of interest
  • Data and confidentiality
  • Speak-up moments

Facilitation note

Psychological safety matters. The goal is decision practice, not public shaming of past mistakes.
