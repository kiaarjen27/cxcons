HRDC In-House Training Claim Evidence Checklist

In-house programs are flexible — but claims still need a complete evidence trail. Use this checklist to keep every session submission-ready.

Why in-house claims need tighter control

In-house training often involves internal scheduling, mixed participant lists, and multiple cost components. That flexibility is useful operationally, but it increases the chance of missing documents at claim stage.

A fixed evidence checklist reduces dependency on memory and helps finance, HR, and the training provider work from the same file standard.

Core evidence to prepare

  • Approved grant / course reference matching the delivered program
  • Final participant list with attendance records for each day
  • Trainer details consistent with the approved arrangement
  • Tax invoice and proof of payment for claimable fee items
  • Venue / mode confirmation where relevant to the approved setup
  • Supporting cost documents only for items allowed under current ACM rules

Before you submit

Cross-check names, dates, and program title across the grant, invoice, and attendance sheet. Small mismatches are one of the most common causes of delay.

If attendance is partial, understand proration impact before filing. Do not assume full claim value when participation records are incomplete.

Operational tip for recurring in-house runs

Create one master checklist template for all in-house programs. After each session, close the file within a fixed number of working days so claim preparation does not pile up at month-end or quarter-end.
