Train-to-Hire: Future Workers Training

The Future Workers Training (FWT) scheme is your strategic "train-to-hire" solution. It enables you to equip potential candidates with exact job-ready skills before they officially join your payroll.

With the latest ACM updates, employers can claim up to RM10,500/day/group for in-house FWT programs. This allows you to scale your recruitment and upskilling efforts simultaneously, minimizing onboarding time.

We assist you in structuring FWT programs that meet HRDC verification standards, ensuring your training content and trainer profiles are fully aligned for grant approval. Build a workforce that is ready from Day One.
