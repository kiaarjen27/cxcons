Claiming for E-Learning and Remote Online Training

Remote Online Training (ROT) is generally treated in line with face-to-face programmes for claim structure, while pure e-learning follows a distinct hourly matrix. Knowing which category you are in — before you run the session — protects the claim file.

Industry Pulse · HRD Corp claims

Online delivery is normal for many Malaysian employers. Claim risk rises when e-learning, ROT, and physical training are mixed in the same file without matching evidence rules.

E-learning: hourly matrix logic

  • For e-learning programmes of seven hours or less, employers key a decimal day value into e-TRiS (commonly 0.1 for 1 hour up to 1.0 for 7 hours)
  • A maximum in the order of RM125 per hour per participant is often referenced for eligible e-learning hours (e.g. up to RM875 for a full day at that rate) — always confirm the current ACM / circular rates
  • Longer programmes are calculated by adding half-day (0.5) or full-day (1.0) increments as applicable
  • A minimum of one complete module and one hour is typically required

Remote Online Training (ROT)

  • ROT — whether in-house or public — generally follows the same fee, allowance, and supporting-document logic as the corresponding face-to-face category
  • Do not assume “online” automatically means the e-learning matrix; classification matters
  • System-generated attendance reports (with timestamps, trainee names, and dual signatures plus company stamps where required) are mandatory for many online claims

Allowances and attendance

  • Employers may claim only one type of allowance (meal or trainee) where rules require a single choice
  • At least 75% attendance is commonly required for full entitlement; otherwise amounts are prorated
  • Keep the attendance trail aligned to what e-TRiS and the claim pack will show

What to do next

Before the programme runs, confirm whether the delivery is pure e-learning or ROT, which rate table applies, and which attendance evidence will be accepted. Assign one owner for the claim pack and one review after the session — incomplete online evidence is a frequent rejection trigger.

Bottom line

Classify the mode correctly, collect the right attendance proof, and follow the current ACM / circular rates. All approvals remain at HRD Corp’s sole discretion and are subject to the prevailing rate of financial assistance.
