Outdoor Team Building: Safety First Design

Energy is valuable. Uncontrolled risk is not culture — it is negligence.

Safety is part of experience quality

Clients remember injuries longer than they remember a photo wall. Screening, briefings, and stop authority protect people and the employer.

Non-negotiables

  • Health screening questions before high-intensity activities
  • Weather postponement / modification rule agreed in advance
  • Trained first aid coverage proportionate to activity risk
  • Difficulty levels communicated early (including opt-down options)
  • Facilitator stop authority that leadership will back

Brief participants like adults

Explain risks, hydration, footwear, and how to signal stop. Inclusion means offering roles with lower physical load — not pressuring everyone into the same intensity.
