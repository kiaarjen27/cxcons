Anti-Bribery Awareness: Make the Grey Areas Discussable

Most integrity failures start in small grey zones — gifts, favours, and pressure to “just process it.” Awareness training gives staff language and boundaries before a serious breach.

Situations staff actually face

  • Festival gifts with expectations attached
  • Vendors asking for faster approvals
  • Travel or entertainment that looks personal
  • Pressure to bypass controls for a preferred party

What training should enable

Staff should know the organisation’s stance, where to report concerns, and how to refuse without escalating conflict alone.

Governance benefit

Documented awareness sessions support a culture argument: the company communicated standards, not only published a policy PDF.
