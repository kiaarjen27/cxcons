Team Building Focused on Communication

Many team failures are communication failures under time pressure. Activities that surface listening habits and message clarity transfer better than pure physical challenge alone.

Communication outcomes to target

  • Listen before rebutting
  • Make requests specific
  • Escalate issues earlier
  • Close loops after handovers

Debrief questions that matter

Where did messages break? Who needed information they did not get? What norm would prevent a repeat?

Pair with skill modules

Conflict resolution and presentation or writing skills can reinforce the same theme after the team day.

Plan

State communication as the primary outcome in your request.
