Essential Rules for Successful HRD Corp Claims

Even when costs fall within ACM ceilings, claims can be rejected or reduced if attendance, allowance or documentation rules are not followed. These operational rules apply across almost every scheme.

Attendance must reach at least 75 % of total training hours; otherwise fees and allowances are prorated. Employers may claim only one type of allowance—either meal or trainee allowance—depending on the training location. Half-day rates apply when training lasts only four hours.

When a training provider submits the claim, Joint Declaration Form JD14, Attendance T3 Form and (for online programmes) a signed system-generated attendance report are compulsory. Air tickets require ticket evidence plus an invoice from the travel agent. HRD Corp may request any additional documents for verification and reserves the right to revise the ACM at any time. All final approvals rest solely with HRD Corp.
