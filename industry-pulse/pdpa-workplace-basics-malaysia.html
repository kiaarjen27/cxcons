PDPA at Work: Data Care Is Part of HR Practice

Employee files, candidate applications, and training records all involve personal data. PDPA awareness helps Malaysian employers reduce careless sharing and weak storage habits.

Where workplace data risk shows up

  • CVs left in open shared drives
  • WhatsApp groups used for IC numbers and medical notes
  • Training lists sent to the wrong distribution list
  • Ex-employee access not removed on time

Practical habits for HR teams

Collect only what you need, limit access by role, and set a clear rule for how long records are kept. If a third party processes data for you, know what they hold.

Training angle

Short awareness sessions for HR, admin, and supervisors reduce everyday leakage more effectively than a policy document alone.
