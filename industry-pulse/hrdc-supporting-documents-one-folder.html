One Folder Rule for HRDC Supporting Documents

Claims fail in email threads. One controlled folder per programme run is operational hygiene. Shared inboxes and personal drives create competing versions of attendance, invoices, and supporting records.

Industry Pulse · HRD Corp compliance

When a claim query arrives from HRD Corp, organisations often spend days reconstructing what should have been a single, streamlined audit trail. Fragmented document management creates version confusion and missing paperwork risks.

Folder minimum requirements

  • Programme outline — exact course structure and content actually delivered
  • Attendance records — complete, dated, and identifiable participant logs
  • Financial trail — official invoices and payment proof documentation
  • Trainer credentials — trainer profile and type notes if required by the scheme
  • Exception documentation — records covering withdrawals, short hours, or participant replacements

Standardised naming conventions

Use a consistent format such as programme + date + company in the folder name so finance and HR teams can locate files months later during audits. Close and lock the folder within seven days of the final session day while memory and session logs are still accurate.

Operational habits to adopt

  • Assign a single administrative owner for each programme folder
  • Enforce clean digital packaging from external training providers before final sign-off
  • Align internal HR and finance verification checkpoints
  • Archive complete runs immediately following claim submission

What to do next

Translate these folder rules into one clear protocol shared across your L&D, HR, and finance teams. If administrative staff are storing documents across scattered personal drives, centralise your filing framework now to avoid submission delays.

Bottom line

Clean documentation is the backbone of successful training claims. Enforcing the one-folder rule turns chaotic email follow-ups into a predictable, audit-ready routine.
