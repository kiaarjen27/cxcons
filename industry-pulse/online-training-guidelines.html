Guidelines for Online Training

HRDF’s Online Training Guideline (Version 4.0) consolidates schemes supporting e-learning, mobile learning, and remote online training. It ensures training quality even when delivered virtually.

Remote Online Training requires platforms with login monitoring, document sharing, two-way communication, and activity reports. HRDF officers must be given access for inspection at least 24 hours before training.

Employers can claim up to RM700 per pax per day for online programmes, with consumable material and internet data costs also covered. Attendance reports and video records must be kept for two years.
