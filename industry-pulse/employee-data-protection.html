Are You a "Data User"? HR’s Legal Identity

If your HR department holds, uses, or controls employee records, you are a "Data User" under the PDPA. Understanding this legal identity is the first step toward avoiding severe penalties.

Section 4 defines a "data user" as a person who processes personal data or has control over it. For companies, this applies to the legal entity, meaning HR leadership is directly responsible for maintaining the register of data users (Section 20) and compliance with all seven Personal Data Protection Principles.
