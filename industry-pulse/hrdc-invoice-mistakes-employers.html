Invoice Mistakes That Delay HRDC Claims

The invoice is not a formality. Mismatch between invoice, attendance, and programme record is a classic delay trigger.

Three documents must tell the same story

Invoice, attendance, and programme outline should describe the same training event — same dates, same audience logic, and a commercial structure a reviewer can follow.

Errors that create queries

  • Training dates that do not match attendance sheets
  • Line items that cannot be mapped to allowable categories
  • Wrong billing entity or incomplete supplier details
  • Bundled “training package” costs with no readable breakdown when breakdown is needed
  • Headcount on the invoice that does not match who signed in

Working habit

Review a draft invoice pattern with your provider before training week, not after submission pressure starts. Ask: if a stranger opened only the invoice, attendance, and outline — would they recognise one event?
