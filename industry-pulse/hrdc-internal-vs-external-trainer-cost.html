Internal vs External Trainer: Cost Logic Employers Miss

Trainer type is not only a quality choice. It changes how costs are reasoned in claim planning.

Confirm trainer type before you lock the budget story

Employers sometimes price a programme as external delivery while the claim file is built on internal-trainer assumptions — or the reverse. That mismatch shows up later as queries, cuts, or internal blame between HR and finance.

Practical checks

  • Who is facilitating: internal staff or external provider?
  • Which cost lines are being proposed as claimable?
  • Does the outline match the trainer model?
  • Are supporting documents consistent with that model?

Planning tip

Write trainer type on the training request form so procurement, HR, and finance use the same assumption from day one.
