Investing in Expertise: Professional Certification Courses

Professional Certification remains one of the most effective ways to validate workforce expertise. With the updated ACM terms, HRD Corp provides a streamlined path for employers to claim costs for recognized certification programs.

Unlike General Courses, Professional Certification Courses under HRD Corp are claimable "as per charged." This removes the typical daily group cost caps, allowing employers to pursue high-quality, industry-recognized credentials for their staff.

Grant applications for these courses must include detailed trainer profiles and course content, which are prorated based on attendance completion. This ensures that the financial assistance provided by HRD Corp is directly tied to the successful attainment of professional standards by the employees.
