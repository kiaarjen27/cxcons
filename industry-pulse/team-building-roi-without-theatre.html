Team Building ROI Without Theatre

You do not need a fake formula. You need a named outcome and visible signals afterward.

Return starts as clarity

If the objective is vague (“bonding”), measurement will be vague. If the objective is behavioural (“cleaner handoffs between sales and ops under time pressure”), observation is possible.

A simple four-step ROI logic

  • Name the Monday behaviour you want
  • Design activities and debrief around it
  • Agree 2–3 observable signals with the sponsoring manager
  • Review at 30 days — not only at the closing circle

Examples of observable signals

  • Fewer escalations caused by missing information on handoff forms
  • Cross-department meeting ends with named owners, not “we’ll follow up”
  • New hires report they know whom to ask within two weeks
