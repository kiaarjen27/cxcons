New Standards in Occupational Safety

The Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 (Act 514) establishes a "duty of care" for everyone in the workplace. Recent comprehensive amendments (Act A1648), effective June 1, 2024, have significantly strengthened compliance requirements and expanded protections for all Malaysian workers.

The 2024 update introduced Section 26A, which empowers employees with the right to remove themselves from a work situation if they have reasonable justification to believe there is an "imminent danger." Furthermore, Section 18B now mandates that all employers and principals must conduct and implement formal risk assessments to proactively manage workplace hazards before accidents occur.

Penalties for non-compliance have seen a massive increase to deter negligence; the maximum fine for a breach of general duties has risen from RM50,000 to RM500,000. This shift moves the industry away from a prescriptive "check-list" mentality toward a philosophy of self-regulation, where the responsibility for a safe environment is embedded into the core operations of every business.
