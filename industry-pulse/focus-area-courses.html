Driving Talent via Focus Area Courses

As HR leaders, you are tasked with future-proofing your workforce. HRDC’s "Focus Area" courses are your most powerful tool to incentivize high-level technical and strategic training at a lower net cost.

With categories ranging from Industry 4.0 to Future Technology and Micro-credentials, these courses are verified by the Industry Expert Committee. They aren't just training—they are certified markers of workforce evolution.

We assist HR departments in identifying which Focus Area courses align with their annual training needs and help coordinate the verification process. Don’t settle for generic training; use the Focus Area framework to elevate your team’s expertise.
