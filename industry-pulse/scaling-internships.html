Scaling Internships: The Enhanced ITS

HRD Corp has overhauled the Industrial Training Scheme (ITS), increasing levy allocation to 50% and extending internship durations to 12 months. It's the perfect time to build your future talent pipeline.

The enhanced ITS allows employers to claim monthly allowances, PPE, and insurance for trainees. By utilizing your HRDC levy, you can provide high-quality placements that bridge the gap between academic theory and industrial practice.

At CXCONS, we help you navigate the documentation—from university agreements to claim submission—ensuring your internship programs are fully compliant and optimized for maximum levy reimbursement.
