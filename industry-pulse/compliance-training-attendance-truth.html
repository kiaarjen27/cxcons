Compliance Training Attendance Must Be True

If someone else signs in for mandatory compliance learning, the organisation is lying to itself.

False completion is a control failure

Especially for safety, harassment, and anti-bribery topics, presence matters. A certificate without the person creates a dangerous story: leadership thinks the risk is covered.

Hardening completion integrity

  • Identity checks proportionate to risk
  • No blanket proxy signatures
  • Manager accountability for team completion truth
  • Refreshers scheduled before certificates become empty artefacts

Culture signal

How leadership reacts when a false completion is found teaches the organisation whether compliance is real or decorative.
