Training Effectiveness: Beyond Attendance Sheets

Attendance proves presence. Effectiveness needs a second layer — whether behaviour or process improved after the session. Keep measurement simple enough that managers will actually use it.

Lightweight signals that work

  • Supervisor observation 2–4 weeks after training
  • One process metric tied to the skill taught
  • Participant application notes in follow-up meetings
  • Output assessment style before/after ratings where relevant

Avoid overbuilding

Complex ROI models collapse when data is incomplete. Start with one outcome per program and review it quarterly.

Link to design

If you cannot name the expected change before training, measurement will fail after training. Write the outcome into the brief.

CXCons tools

Use structured program design, OA forms where appropriate, and TNA priorities so evaluation connects to the original gap.
