Outsourced HR Services and the PDPA

Does your HR team use third-party payroll or cloud-based benefits platforms? Under the PDPA, if you are a "data user," you are accountable for how your "data processors" handle employee information.

Section 9(2) is critical for HR managers. It states that if you outsource data processing, you must ensure the data processor provides "sufficient guarantees" regarding security measures and compliance. You cannot outsource your legal liability.
