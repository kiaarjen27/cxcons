Harassment Reporting Channels Must Be Usable

A policy PDF is not a channel. People need a believable path that does not punish the reporter.

Trust determines usage

If employees believe reporting ends careers, formal systems become decorative. Channel design is only half the work — the first manager or HR response decides whether the next person speaks.

Design checks

  • More than one reporting route (not only “tell your boss”)
  • Confidentiality boundaries explained in plain language
  • Response timelines that are realistic and kept
  • Protection against retaliation stated and enforced

First-response script for managers

  • Listen without interrogating like an investigator if policy says escalate
  • Do not promise outcomes you cannot control
  • Protect confidentiality — no corridor narration
  • Document factually and involve HR early
