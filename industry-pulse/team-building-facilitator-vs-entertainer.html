Facilitator vs Entertainer in Team Building

Entertainment creates noise. Facilitation creates meaning and agreements.

Ask who will run the debrief

If nobody can lead reflection, you bought activity — not development. Photos can look identical; transfer does not.

Facilitation markers

  • Clear objectives stated before games begin
  • Structured debrief questions (what happened → so what → now what)
  • Psychological safety so quieter voices can speak
  • Commitments captured with owners — not only applause

When entertainment-only is the wrong buy

  • Post-restructure trust work
  • Sales–ops conflict
  • Leadership alignment offsites
  • Any brief that includes “improve collaboration metrics”
