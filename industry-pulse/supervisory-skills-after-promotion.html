Promoted to Supervisor: The Skills Gap Nobody Budgets For

High individual contributors are often promoted for technical excellence. The new job is different: people, priorities, and process. Without support, teams feel the gap quickly.

What new supervisors typically lack

  • How to give corrective feedback early
  • Delegation without dumping
  • Handling conflict before it becomes formal
  • Documenting issues fairly

Business cost of skipping this

Team friction rises, good staff disengage, and HR receives late escalations that were preventable at supervisor level.

A practical first pathway

Blend leadership essentials, communication, and employment basics. Keep it short, applied, and scenario-based so supervisors can use tools the next day.

Plan it properly

Run a light TNA for the supervisory layer, then shortlist modules through Program Finder or a custom in-house request.
