Mental Health at Work: From Awareness to Manager Response

Workplaces that only talk about productivity eventually pay for unmanaged stress. Awareness training helps staff recognise signals; manager training helps the organisation respond early and respectfully.

Why this is an employer issue

Stress, burnout, and untreated strain show up as errors, conflict, absence, and turnover. Waiting for a crisis is more expensive than building basic literacy.

What useful training covers

  • Recognising early changes in behaviour and performance
  • Supportive conversation skills for managers
  • Boundaries between support and clinical care
  • Practical stress-management habits for teams

Keep it practical

Avoid one-off posters with no manager pathway. Pair awareness sessions with clear internal referral points and follow-up habits.
