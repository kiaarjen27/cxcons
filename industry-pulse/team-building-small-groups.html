Team Building for Small Groups (8–20)

Small groups do not need stadium logistics. They need tighter facilitation, deeper conversation, and activities that do not feel padded.

What works better at small scale

  • Longer debriefs
  • Higher personal accountability in tasks
  • Honest discussion of working norms
  • Venues that allow private conversation

What to avoid

Formats that depend on large-number noise or excessive waiting time.

Budget reality

Per-person cost may read higher than mass packages; impact density is usually better.

Request

State headcount clearly so proposed formats stay proportionate.
