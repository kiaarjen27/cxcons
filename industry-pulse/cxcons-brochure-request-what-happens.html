What Happens After a Brochure Request

A brochure request is not a shopping cart checkout. It starts a fit conversation — outline, duration, audience, and delivery options — so you receive something useful, not a generic PDF sent into the void.

Industry Pulse · Working with CXCons

When you submit a request through Program Finder or a module page, our team reviews the module title and your contact details, then follows up so the outline matches how you intend to run the programme (public-style interest, in-house, or still exploring).

What we typically clarify

  • Programme fit — standard module vs customised version
  • Duration and format — 1 day / 2 day, in-person or other delivery needs
  • Audience — role level and approximate headcount
  • Timing — target month or urgency
  • HRD Corp angle — if claim structure is part of your planning

What helps you get a faster, better reply

  • Accurate work email — so outlines and follow-ups reach you
  • Headcount hint — even “about 15–20” is enough to frame delivery
  • Timing preference — this quarter / next quarter / exploring only
  • Company name — helps us avoid mixing requests and keep context

What a request is not

  • Not an automatic booking
  • Not a binding quotation until scope and date are confirmed
  • Not limited to one module — you can request several titles as you shortlist

Bottom line

A brochure request starts a fit conversation. Clear contact details and a simple hint on headcount and timing help us return the right outline, duration options, and next step — without unnecessary back-and-forth.
