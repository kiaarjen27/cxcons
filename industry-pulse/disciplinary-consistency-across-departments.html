Disciplinary Consistency Across Departments

Similar cases with different outcomes become organisational stories that destroy credibility.

Calibrate before you conclude

HR should help managers compare like cases and policy ranges. Lone-wolf sanctions teach politics, not standards.

Consistency tools

  • Sanction guidance ranges
  • Case review for serious actions
  • Training for managers on evidence standards
  • Documented reasons when departing from precedent

What staff hear

“In Department A you get a warning; in Department B you get dismissed for the same thing” travels faster than any values poster.
