ALAT Themes: Modernising Internal Learning

ALAT is HRD Corp’s scheme for training aids and training-room setup — not a general office renovation grant, and not a substitute for training course claims.

What ALAT is for

ALAT (Training Facilities & Renovation) helps registered employers purchase training aids and set up, renovate, or enhance training rooms so they can run more in-house retraining and upskilling.

It sits beside course schemes (such as HCC / SBL). ALAT is about the facility and equipment layer that makes structured internal learning practical — projectors, room fit-out within allowed categories, and training-only equipment relevant to your business.

Financial assistance employers should know

  • Assistance is subject to a maximum of 50% of the levy balance as at 1 January in the year you apply
  • That 50% figure is the eligibility ceiling enhancement under Employer Circular No. 3/2024 (previously commonly referenced at 30%)
  • Apply via e-TRiS under the ALAT scheme
  • Application must be submitted before purchase of equipment or start of renovation
  • Purchase should be completed within 6 months from approval date
  • Employers should have no legal issues with HRD Corp when applying

What can typically be supported

Training aids / equipment

  • Used solely for training
  • Relevant to the employer’s nature of business
  • Placed in the training room

Training room setup (limited categories)

  • Overhead projector wiring
  • Wall painting
  • Air-conditioner setup
  • Carpet, blinds, glass wall installation
  • Ceiling renovation
  • Electrical outlet wiring
  • Network and internet connection installations
  • Installation costs tied to equipment under the same grant

What ALAT is not

  • Construction of a building or training centre
  • Rental of training rooms
  • Non-training equipment or general office items
  • Software (generally not funded under ALAT)
  • Standalone labour / installation-only / calibration-only requests without qualifying purchase logic
  • Equipment used for day-to-day business operations (must remain training-only)

Worked eligibility idea (simplified)

If levy balance as at 1 January is RM10,000, eligibility under the 50% rule is up to RM5,000 for that year (subject to remaining eligibility at approval and HRD Corp’s assessment).

Applying for more than the eligibility amount does not expand the ceiling — approval is still capped by eligibility and scheme rules.

Planning sequence that avoids rework

  • 1. Define the internal learning use-case — which roles, which modules, how often the room will be used
  • 2. Separate ALAT from course claims — equipment vs trainer/course fees under ACM / HCC / SBL
  • 3. Build a training-only equipment list — reject mixed-use items early
  • 4. Apply in e-TRiS before buying — do not purchase first and hope
  • 5. Keep invoices and breakdowns — detailed receipts matter at claim stage
  • 6. Place and use assets in the training room — location and purpose should match the application

Shared / industry training locations

Enhancements referenced by HRD Corp also allow, in relevant cases, equipment or room support in industry training locations (for example shared venues such as industrial training institutes), so facilities can benefit current and future workers more widely. Treat this as a structured application path — not an informal workaround — and follow current HRD Corp instructions for shared venues.

How ALAT connects to your wider training plan

  • ALAT — room and training aids
  • Course schemes + ACM — programme fees, allowances, attendance logic
  • Internal curriculum — what will actually be taught in the room after the fit-out

A renovated room without a learning plan becomes expensive storage. Pair facility investment with clear in-house pathways and claim-aware delivery habits.
