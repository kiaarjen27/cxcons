Whistleblowing Channels Need Credible Protection

People test whether the organisation protects reporters. One retaliation story can shut the channel down.

Credibility is operational

Triage, confidentiality, and leadership restraint determine whether the channel is real. Posters without protection are theatre.

Design markers

  • Independent triage where proportionate
  • Anti-retaliation enforcement examples leaders will stand behind
  • Clear scope of what the channel covers
  • Feedback to reporters where possible without compromising process

Leadership test

How leaders speak about a reporter in private meetings is the real policy. Staff notice tone faster than they read handbooks.
