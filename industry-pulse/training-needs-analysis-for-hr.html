Training Needs Analysis: Prioritise Before You Buy Training

Random course shopping wastes budget. A simple TNA gives HR a defensible view of priority gaps, likely modules, and a sequence managers can support.

What a practical TNA answers

  • Which capability gaps hurt performance now
  • Which teams are most affected
  • What should be trained this quarter versus later
  • Which format fits — public, in-house, or blended

Keep V1 simple

You do not need a complex platform on day one. A structured set of questions, a priority list, and mapped program suggestions already improve decision quality.

Link to action

After priorities are clear, move into Program Finder brochure requests or a custom pathway — not another year of unfocused nominations.
