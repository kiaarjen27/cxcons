Campaign Terms & Conditions

These terms and conditions apply to selected HRDC-related incentive and appreciation campaigns run by Circlex Consultancy (CXCons). They outline who can participate, how rewards are issued, and the rules that govern qualification and redemption.

Campaign rewards are a professional appreciation initiative and are separate from official HRD Corp claimable training costs.

Eligibility

  • Applies only to HRDC-funded programs under active campaign terms.

Reward Issuance
