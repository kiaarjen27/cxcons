Campaign Terms & Conditions

General Framework for HRDC Incentive & Appreciation Campaigns.

📋 Eligibility

  • Applies only to HRDC-funded programs.
  • Must meet the minimum investment threshold per campaign.
  • Training and registration must fall within specified deadlines.

🎁 Reward Issuance

  • Issued only after HRDC claim approval and payment.
  • Non-transferable and non-exchangeable for cash.
  • Subject to the specific redemption policies of the issuer.

Rules of Participation

Qualification Conditions

Submissions must be 100% error-free. Any mistakes in HRDC registration or claim details will result in automatic disqualification from the incentive program.

Redemption Policy

Rewards are distributed following training execution. The organizer is not liable for lost, stolen, or expired vouchers once they have been officially issued.

General Legal Terms

This campaign is a professional appreciation initiative and does not form part of the official HRDC claimable costs. We reserve the right to amend or terminate campaigns with prior notice.
